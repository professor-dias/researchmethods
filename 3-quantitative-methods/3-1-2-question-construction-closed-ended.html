Question Construction

The format of a survey question determines the type of variable you will work with. Try each one below.

Closed-Ended Question Formats

Have you ever been diagnosed with depression?
Produces: Binary
Which best describes your employment status?
Produces: Nominal (unordered)
Rate your satisfaction from 1 to 5.
Produces: Ordinal (ordered)
I feel anxious about public speaking.
Strongly DisagreeStrongly Agree
Produces: Ordinal (Likert item)
How would you describe this product?
WeakStrong
Produces: Ordinal (semantic differential)
How many cigarettes did you smoke in the past 7 days?
Produces: Discrete / Count
