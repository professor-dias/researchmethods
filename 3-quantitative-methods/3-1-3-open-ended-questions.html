Open-Ended Questions

Open-ended questions produce free-text responses. These require content analysis, thematic analysis, or natural language processing (NLP) before they can be quantitatively analyzed.

Raw response
"Tell us about your experience with the healthcare system."
"I waited almost three hours just to be seen, but once I got in, the nurse was incredibly kind and thorough. Honestly the bill afterward was a shock — nobody warned me what it would cost."
Coded themes
Access & Wait Times
Provider Satisfaction
Cost Transparency

A researcher (or an NLP model) reads responses like this across the whole sample and tags recurring ideas — turning free text into countable categories.

Once responses are coded this way, "theme" becomes a nominal variable — you can count how many respondents mentioned cost, or cross-tabulate themes against other variables. But the coding step itself is interpretive, which is why qualitative rigor (double-coding, inter-rater reliability) matters.
