Deductive angle

Problem statement: A prior exploratory look suggests Drug A has a high response rate — but is that meaningfully better than what we'd expect without treatment?
Research question: Does Drug A work better than a placebo (a sugar pill with no active medicine)?
Null hypothesis (H0): Drug A works no better than a placebo — patients would improve about as often even without the real drug.
Alternative hypothesis (H1): Drug A works better than a placebo — it improves symptoms more often than an inactive pill would.

The yardstick here is the placebo response rate, not "no treatment at all" or "another drug." For this illustration we assume that placebo yardstick is 50%.
