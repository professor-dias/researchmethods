The Statistical Framework

Every statistical method exists to answer one plain question. Here's the whole map at once — question, category, and every test that lives under it. The highlighted tests are the ones health science grad students reach for most often — start there, and let the rest fade into "good to know exists." Click any row to jump to the full deep-dive in Topic 4.

What do I have?Describe
Descriptive statistics, Frequencies, Percentages, Crosstabs, Confidence intervals (descriptive), Standard deviation, Variance, Standard error, Histograms, Boxplots, Density plots, QQ plots, Normality tests (Shapiro-Wilk, Kolmogorov-Smirnov)
Are they different?Compare
Independent t-test, Paired t-test, One-way ANOVA, Two-way ANOVA, Repeated Measures ANOVA, ANCOVA, MANOVA, MANCOVA, Mann–Whitney U, Wilcoxon Signed-Rank, Kruskal–Wallis, Friedman Test, Mood's Median Test, and post-hoc tests: Tukey, Bonferroni, Holm, Games-Howell, Dunnett
Are they related?Relate
Pearson correlation, Spearman correlation, Kendall's Tau, Chi-square, Fisher's Exact, McNemar, Cochran's Q, Cramer's V, Phi, Odds Ratio, Relative Risk
Can I predict it?Predict
Linear Regression, Multiple Regression, Polynomial Regression, Logistic Regression, Ordinal Logistic Regression, Multinomial Logistic Regression, Poisson Regression, Negative Binomial Regression, Zero-Inflated Models, Cox Regression, Parametric Survival Models, Generalized Linear Models (GLMs), Generalized Additive Models (GAMs)
Why does it happen?Explain
Mediation, Moderation, Moderated Mediation, Conditional Process Analysis, Path Analysis, Structural Equation Modeling (SEM)
Am I measuring it right?Measure
Cronbach's Alpha, McDonald's Omega, KR-20, ICC, Exploratory Factor Analysis (EFA), Confirmatory Factor Analysis (CFA), Item Response Theory (IRT), Rasch Models, Convergent validity, Discriminant validity, Criterion validity
Are there hidden groups?Discover
K-Means, Hierarchical Clustering, DBSCAN, Gaussian Mixture Models, Latent Class Analysis (LCA), Latent Profile Analysis (LPA), Latent Transition Analysis, Principal Components Analysis (PCA), Factor Analysis, t-SNE, UMAP
How does it change over time?Longitudinal
Repeated Measures ANOVA, Linear Mixed Models, Generalized Linear Mixed Models, GEE, Growth Curve Modeling, Latent Growth Modeling, Time Series Analysis, ARIMA
Did this cause that?Causal Inference
Propensity Score Matching, Exact Matching, Mahalanobis Matching, IPTW / IPW, Standardization, Difference-in-Differences, Interrupted Time Series, Regression Discontinuity, Instrumental Variables
Can a computer learn the pattern?Machine Learning
Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, XGBoost, SVM, Neural Networks, KNN, Naive Bayes, K-Means, Hierarchical Clustering, PCA, Autoencoders
How should new evidence change my belief?Bayesian
Bayesian Regression, Bayesian t-test, Bayesian ANOVA, Bayesian SEM, MCMC, Bayes Factors, Hierarchical Bayesian Models
Can I trust this model?Diagnostics
Normality, Homoscedasticity, Linearity, Independence, Multicollinearity, Influential observations, R², Adjusted R², RMSE, MAE, AIC, BIC, Hosmer–Lemeshow, ROC Curve / AUC, Calibration plots
What about missing information?Missing Data
Complete Case Analysis, Mean/Median Imputation, Hot Deck, Regression Imputation, Multiple Imputation, MICE, FIML, EM Algorithm
How do I avoid false alarms?Multiple Testing
Bonferroni, Holm, Hochberg, Benjamini-Hochberg (FDR), Benjamini-Yekutieli, Šidák
Notice Describe and Measure come first for a reason: look at your data honestly, and make sure you're measuring the right thing, before you ever compare, relate, or predict anything. The full deep-dive on every row above lives in Topic 4 · Statistical Modeling.
