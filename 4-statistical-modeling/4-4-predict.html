Predict

The question: If I know one thing about someone, can I make a good guess about something else?

This goes one step further than Relate: instead of just noticing a pattern, you build a formula that turns the pattern into a specific prediction.

Flagship tool: Linear Regression
Why we do this: You want a formula that predicts a number (like blood pressure) from another number (like age).
The logic: Draw the one straight line that stays closest to all your dots. That line is your prediction machine — plug in an X value, read off the predicted Y.
What to look for: How steep is the line, and how closely do the dots hug it?
What it means: A steep line means a small change in X comes with a big change in Y. Dots hugging the line tightly mean predictions will usually land close to correct; a scattered cloud means the formula is barely better than a guess.
