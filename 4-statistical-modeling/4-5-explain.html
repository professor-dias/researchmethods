Explain

The question: I know two things are connected — but why? What's happening in between?

Relate and Predict tell you THAT something is connected. Explain tries to open the box and show the mechanism in between.

Flagship tool: Mediation
Why we do this: You suspect X affects Y only because X first affects something else, M, which then affects Y.
The logic: Test three connections at once: does X affect M? Does M affect Y? And once M is accounted for, does X still affect Y directly, or does its effect shrink?
What to look for: Whether the direct X→Y effect shrinks once M is added to the model.
What it means: Example: exercise (X) improves mood (Y) — but mostly because exercise improves sleep (M), and better sleep improves mood.

If path a and path b are both strong, and the direct path c′ shrinks once M is added, M explains most of the story.

Related tools

ToolIn plain words
ModerationTesting whether the X→Y effect changes depending on a third variable (exercise helps mood more for younger people than older)
Moderated MediationCombining both: the "why" story itself changes depending on who you look at
Conditional Process AnalysisA general toolkit combining mediation and moderation in one model
Path AnalysisMapping several connected effects across many variables at once
Structural Equation Modeling (SEM)The full toolkit: path analysis plus hidden, unmeasured constructs (see Measure)
