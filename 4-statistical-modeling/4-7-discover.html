Discover

The question: Are there hidden groups or patterns in my data that I didn't know to look for?

Every method so far assumed you already knew what groups to test. Discover flips that: let the data show you the groups.

Flagship tool: Clustering
Why we do this: You suspect your people naturally split into different types, but you don't know how many types or who belongs where.
The logic: Plot everyone as a dot based on their answers. Group together the dots that sit close to each other. Each tight little cloud of dots becomes one "type."
What to look for: Do clear, separate clumps of dots appear, or does everything blend into one blob?
What it means: Clear clumps suggest real, distinct subgroups worth naming and studying separately. A blob means everyone is more alike than different.
