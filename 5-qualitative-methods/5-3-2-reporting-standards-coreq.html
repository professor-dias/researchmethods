The Checklist Reviewers Already Have Open

2.4 gave you a checklist for critiquing someone else's paper. This page gives you the checklist for writing your own — COREQ (Consolidated Criteria for Reporting Qualitative Research), the standard many health-science journals require before they'll even send a qualitative manuscript out for review.

COREQ organizes everything this whole topic has covered into three domains, in the order a reader actually needs them: who did the research and how they positioned themselves, how the study was designed and run, and how the analysis was actually done. Below is a working version of that checklist — open each domain when you're ready to write or review that part of your paper.
